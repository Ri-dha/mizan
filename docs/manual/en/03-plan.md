## Reading the screen

The top card lists every bucket for the selected month with allocated, committed, spent and free. Use the arrows to move between months.

## Changing the split

Edit names, colours and percentages in the **Allocation** card. Percentages take one decimal place. The bar underneath tells you whether the shares add up to 100% and, if not, by how many dinars you are over or under this month's planned income. **Rebalance** scales the shares so they add up exactly.

Press **Save plan**. If the plan started this month, it is edited in place. If it started earlier, Mizan closes the old version at the end of last month and starts a new one from the month you are looking at. Earlier months keep the percentages they ran under, so a report from March still shows March's plan.

## Fixed amounts

Some buckets are not a percentage: rent is 600,000 whatever the month brings. Switch on **Fixed amount** and enter it. Fixed buckets are funded first, in their order on the list, and the percentages split whatever income remains. If the fixed amounts add up to more than the planned income, the plan says so and the later buckets receive nothing until the income covers them.

## Carrying over

Switch on **Carry over what is left** for a bucket whose unspent money should survive the month, such as a clothes or repairs fund. What is free at the end of one month is added to the next month's figure, shown separately from the allocation. Carry-over runs within one plan version; saving a new version starts the buckets fresh.

## Moving money

Overspent one bucket? Press **Move money**, pick the source and destination, the amount and a reason. The move is a transfer, not spending: it leaves one bucket and arrives in the other, keeps its reason in this month's list and in the ledger, and can be undone.

## Adding and removing buckets

Add a bucket with **Add bucket**, remove one with the bin icon. A removed bucket disappears from this month onward; its history stays in earlier months.

## When the income is not in yet

Before pay day the allocated column shows zero and the planned share appears in smaller print. Mark the income received on the Income screen and the figures fill in.
