## Sources

Add a source with its amount, currency, and how often it pays: monthly on a given day, every two weeks or weekly from a recent pay date, or once. A source in dollars asks for the rate you expect; each receipt records its own rate on the day.

## This month

The month card lists every expected pay date. When the money arrives press **Mark received**; the amount defaults to the expected one and can be changed. A pay date that has passed without a receipt is shown as not received yet.

**Add irregular income** records a bonus or freelance payment that has no source. It counts toward this month's income and allocation.

## A raise or a cut

Open a source with **Edit** and use **Record a raise or change**: enter the new amount and the date it takes effect. Earlier months keep planning on the old amount; later months use the new one. The amount history shows every step, and a wrong entry can be deleted.

## Ending a source

Press **End** on a source when it stops. It stays in the list, dimmed, and its history remains in earlier months.
