## Adding a bill

A bill has a name, the bucket it comes from, an amount and a frequency: monthly on a due day, quarterly or yearly from a recent due date, or every N days. Switch on **Amount varies** for electricity or the generator; the amount you enter is then an estimate used for planning.

## Due this month

The month card lists every due date with its status. Unpaid bills are **committed** against their bucket from the first day of the month, so the free figure already accounts for them. Overdue bills are highlighted.

## Marking a bill paid

Press **Mark paid**, confirm or correct the amount, and the date. Mizan records the expense in the ledger and settles the obligation in one step. **Undo** reverses both.

## Ending a bill

Open a bill and press **End this bill**. Past months keep it; future months do not.
