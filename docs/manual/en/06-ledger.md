## Quick add

The **+** button is available on every screen. Enter the amount, tap a bucket, press Save. The date defaults to today; payee, category and a note are optional.

## Expense, transfer or income

- **Expense** takes from a bucket and counts as spending.
- **Transfer** moves money from a bucket to a goal, a debt, a cash account or another bucket. It leaves the bucket but is not spending.
- **Income** records money in without a source.

## Reading a receipt

After attaching a photo, press **Read the receipt**. Mizan reads it on the device itself, in Arabic and English, and fills in the total, the date and the shop when it can find them. Check the numbers before saving; a crumpled receipt reads badly. The first read downloads the reader once; after that it works offline. The photo never leaves your phone for this.

## Categories filled in for you

Type the payee and Mizan suggests the category and bucket you used for it before, or a sensible category from the name (a pharmacy, a generator payment, a phone top-up). It only fills fields that are still empty and marks them as suggested; change anything that is wrong and the next suggestion learns from it.

## Splitting a purchase

One supermarket receipt can belong to two buckets. Switch on **Split across buckets**, give each part a bucket and an amount, and save once the remainder reaches zero. Each part is its own entry in its own bucket, marked "Split"; edit or delete them one by one.

## Running costs of an asset

An expense can be marked as a running cost of a car, a generator or any other asset. The asset's card then shows the running costs and the total cost of ownership so far.

## Importing from a CSV file

In Settings under Your data, **Import transactions CSV** reads a bank statement or any spreadsheet. You confirm which column is the date, the amount, the payee and so on, say whether positive amounts are expenses or income, and pick a bucket. Rows that already exist with the same date, amount and payee are marked as duplicates and skipped unless you say otherwise. Nothing is written until you press Import. Give the mapping a name and the next file with the same columns, such as next month's statement from the same bank, opens with it already applied. Direct connections to banks and wallets wait for them to offer customer APIs.

## Finding things

Search by payee, note or category; filter by bucket, type and amount range. Entries are grouped by day for the selected month. Tap an entry to edit it.

## Deleting and restoring

Deleted entries go to a recycle bin for 30 days and can be restored from the bottom of the ledger. After 30 days they are removed for good.

## Receipt photos

Attach a photo from the quick-add sheet. It is downscaled, encrypted on your device and uploaded when there is a connection. Nobody, including the server, can read a receipt without your device's key.

## Closed months

An entry dated in a closed month is refused. Reopen the month on the Net worth screen first.
