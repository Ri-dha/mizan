## Creating a goal

Give the goal a name and a target. Add either a target date, and Mizan tells you the monthly deposit it needs, or a monthly deposit, and Mizan projects the date you reach it. Choose the bucket deposits come from; a planned deposit commits that bucket until the month's deposit is made.

## Deposits and withdrawals

**Deposit** records a transfer from the bucket to the goal. **Withdraw** moves money back. Progress is the sum of the ledger; the last few entries are shown on the card and can be undone.

## Reaching the goal

When deposits reach the target the goal is marked as reached with the date. Archive it from the edit sheet to hide it.
