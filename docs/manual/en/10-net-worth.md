## The number

Net worth is cash, gold and silver at today's price, and money owed to you, minus what you owe. The breakdown shows each class and its share. The dollar line, if switched on in Settings, converts at the rate valuation uses.

## Closing a month

At the end of a month press **Close month**. Mizan asks the server for a snapshot: the totals, the breakdown and every price and rate used. The snapshot never changes. Months also close automatically shortly after they end.

A closed month refuses new entries. **Reopen** if you need to correct something; the reopening is recorded, and closing again takes a fresh snapshot.

## Over time

The chart shows one point per closed month plus today, so you can see whether last month's decisions moved the number.
