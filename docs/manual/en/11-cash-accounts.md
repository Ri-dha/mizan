---
title: "Cash accounts"
screen: /accounts
summary: "Wallet, bank and cash at home as manual balances with a change log."
---
Mizan has no bank connection, so cash is a set of balances you maintain: a wallet, a bank account, cash at home, a mobile wallet. Enter the balance whenever you check it. Each change is logged with the previous and new amounts and an optional reason, so you can see when a balance moved.

Accounts in dollars are converted at the rate valuation uses for the net worth figure. Mark an account **Private to me** to keep it out of other members' lists once households are shared.
