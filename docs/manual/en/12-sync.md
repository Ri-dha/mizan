## How it works

Every change is saved on the device and queued. When there is a connection the queue is sent and the other devices' changes are fetched. The badge at the top shows **Up to date**, the number of changes waiting, or **Offline**.

## Two devices edit the same thing

Each field carries the time of its last change. The newer change wins per field, so a name changed on the phone and a balance changed on the tablet both survive. When your edit loses to a newer one, it is listed under **Overridden edits** with both values so nothing is lost silently.

## Sync now

Press **Sync now** to push and pull immediately. Sync also runs when the app comes back to the foreground and every few minutes while open.
