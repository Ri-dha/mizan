**Why does the plan show zero allocated?** The income has not been marked received yet. Do that on the Income screen.

**I paid a bill but free did not change much.** It was already committed. Paying moves it from committed to spent; free was already net of it.

**My gold shows a loss right after buying.** Making charges are part of what you paid but not of what the metal is worth. The gain excluding making charges is shown next to it.

**Can I change a closed month?** Reopen it on the Net worth screen, make the change, and close it again.

**I lost my phone.** Sign in on the new one; everything comes back from the service. The PIN and receipt keys were on the old phone only; receipts attached there can no longer be decrypted unless they were uploaded while that phone still had them.

**Does Mizan give financial advice?** No. It records and calculates. Payoff comparisons and projections are arithmetic on the numbers you entered.
