## What counts

Anything you own that is worth real money and is not cash, gold or a debt: a car, a flat, land, a laptop, a generator, furniture, livestock. Each one enters net worth at its current value and leaves when you sell it.

## Recording an asset

Choose a type and Mizan asks for the details that matter for it: make, model, year and plate for a vehicle; address and area for property; brand and serial for electronics. Enter what you paid, when, and in which currency. A dollar purchase is converted at the rate on that day and the rate is kept with the asset.

## Current value

An asset is worth its **latest valuation**. When you have never revalued it, the purchase price stands in. Whenever you know better, press **Add valuation** with the amount and the date; the history stays on the card and a wrong entry can be undone.

Assets that lose value over time can also **depreciate** automatically from the latest valuation:

| Method | What happens each month |
|---|---|
| None | Nothing; the value is the latest valuation |
| Straight line | Loses the same amount every month: the yearly rate applied to the baseline value, divided by twelve |
| Declining balance | Loses the yearly rate, divided by twelve, of whatever it is still worth, so the drop shrinks over time |

Both stop at the **salvage value** you set. The card shows which baseline the value comes from and how many months of depreciation have been applied.

## Liquid or illiquid

Property is marked illiquid by default because it cannot usually be turned into cash within a month; everything else is liquid. You can change this per asset. The Net worth screen shows the liquid and illiquid totals side by side.

## When a valuation is old

The home screen reminds you when an asset has gone six months without a fresh valuation. The interval is in Settings.

## Selling

**Sell** records the date and the price. The asset leaves net worth, and the difference between the sale price and the purchase price is shown as the realised gain. Sold assets stay in a collapsed list at the bottom of the screen and a sale can be undone.

## Goals backed by an asset

A goal such as "buy a car" can be backed by the asset once you own it. Progress then follows the asset's value instead of deposits.
