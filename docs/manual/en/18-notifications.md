## Turning them on

In Settings, switch on **Push notifications on this device** and allow the browser's prompt. Each device is enrolled separately, so do it on the phone you carry. **Send a test** confirms the channel works.

On iPhone and iPad, notifications only work once Mizan is installed to the home screen; the browser tab alone cannot receive them.

## What you can be reminded of

| Reminder | When |
|---|---|
| Bill due | A bill's due date is within the lead time (three days by default; each bill can set its own) and it has not been marked paid |
| Pay day | A pay date is today and the income has not been marked received |
| Overspending | Spending in a bucket has reached the threshold share of its allocation (90% by default) |
| Month end | Tomorrow starts a new month and this one is not closed yet |
| Price move | Gold or silver you hold moved more than the threshold since yesterday (2% by default) |

Reminders are worked out once a day on the server, in your language, using only the records you can see: a private bill reminds its owner alone. Each reminder is sent once; running late or reopening the app does not repeat it.

## Quiet mode

**Quiet mode** silences every reminder without forgetting your choices. Switch it off and the same reminders resume.

## Privacy

The push service (Apple, Google or Mozilla, depending on the device) carries an encrypted message it cannot read. Nothing about your finances is stored with it.
