## Monthly report

Planned versus actual per bucket, the biggest categories, the change in spending from last month and the month's saving rate. Use the arrows to move between months.

## The year at a glance

**Year** opens the annual view: income, spending and what was saved for each month, the spending per bucket as a stacked chart, the year's biggest categories, and the change in net worth between the closing snapshots at the start and end of the year. The trailing twelve-month saving rate smooths out a single good or bad month.

## The saving rate

Saving rate = (income received − spending) ÷ income received. Transfers to goals, debts and gold are not spending, so a month in which you bought gold with your salary counts as a month you saved.

## Save as PDF

**Save as PDF** prints the report; choose "Save as PDF" as the printer. The navigation and buttons are left out.

## Spreadsheets

In Settings under Your data, export transactions, gold and silver, or assets as **CSV** or **Excel**. Column names follow the app language, and Arabic sheets open right-to-left. These files are for your own analysis; to move Mizan to another device use the JSON export.
