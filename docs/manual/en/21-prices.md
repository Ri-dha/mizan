**Prices** shows how the dollar, gold and silver have moved. Open it from the menu, or from **Dollar today** on the home screen.

## What you can see

| Price | Unit |
|---|---|
| Dollar, Baghdad market | dinars per dollar |
| Dollar, Central Bank | dinars per dollar |
| Gold 21K | dinars per mithqal (5 grams) |
| Gold 24K | dinars per gram |
| Silver | dinars per gram |
| Gold, world price | dollars per ounce |

Pick a range from a day to everything recorded. A day or a week plots every change; a month or three plot each day's close; a year plots each week's (weeks start on Saturday), and **All** each month's.

Under the chart, **Every change** lists each time the price moved, with what it moved from. A price that is fetched again unchanged is not listed twice.

## Where the prices come from

The service checks the market on a schedule during the day (the dollar every 20 minutes, gold and silver every 30), so changes are recorded even when nobody has the app open. Each price shows its source. The Baghdad dollar comes from IQWealth and is marked **Data: IQWealth**; gold and silver come from Baghdad Bullion House.

History starts when the service began recording. Older dollar prices are not available.

Prices are public information. Everyone in the household can see this page, including dependents and advisors, and none of your own records are involved.
